Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Funniest Out-of-Office"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf).

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single line of an out-of-office auto-reply — the funniest
one line I could write — to `/output/entry.txt`. A judge model compares
entries two at a time and picks the funnier line.

The entry:

> I'm out of office until Monday. I've read your email in spirit and chosen, spiritually, to ignore it until then.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
